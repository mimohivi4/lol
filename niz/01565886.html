How It Works

Nano-Emulsification Technology

Nano-emulsification is the process of breaking down CBD oil into microscopic droplets (10-100 nanometers) and encapsulating them in water-soluble carrier molecules. This technology transforms hydrophobic CBD into a hydrophilic (water-loving) format that dissolves completely in water. The resulting nano-sized particles have an enormous surface area relative to their volume, enabling rapid absorption through the oral mucosa and intestinal lining.

When you drink nano-emulsified CBD water, a significant portion begins absorbing immediately through the mucous membranes in your mouth and throat.
