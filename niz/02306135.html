Are There Any Side Effects or Risks?

Who Should Avoid CBD Gummies?

CBD may not be suitable for everyone. Pregnant or nursing women, people with liver conditions, and those on medication that interacts with CBD should exercise caution. Additionally, anyone with a known allergy to hemp or specific botanical ingredients should read labels carefully.
