What Is PermitPal?

PermitPal is a digital permit guidance tool for homeowners, contractors, investors, and property managers. It helps organize remodel information by combining permit guidance and cost details. The process starts with your project: you enter your ZIP code, describe your remodel, and receive a report that helps you prepare before construction begins. Many homeowners use PermitPal because they want fewer surprises, better planning, and clearer costs.

How PermitPal Works

PermitPal starts with the basics. You know your project type, location, planned changes, and project size, and these details create a clearer permit check. The report may include a municipality-specific permit checklist, estimated permit fees, inspection guidance, material cost estimates, Home Depot and Lowe's links, inspector tips, and common mistake warnings. This helps homeowners understand which permit applies, which rule matters, and which inspection comes next, before materials arrive or workers start.

Why Permit Planning Matters

A remodel budget has many parts, including materials, labor, permit fees, and inspection costs, so a complete budget includes all major costs. Building rules exist to guide safe work, reduce dangerous mistakes, and help inspectors review changes, and skipping a rule does not remove it. Guessing permit needs can create problems, because homeowners may miss required permits, inspection steps, application fees, and local rules, and late fixes usually cost more.
