What Is ProDentim?

ProDentim is a dietary supplement specifically formulated to support the health of your teeth and gums by promoting a healthy balance of beneficial bacteria in the mouth. It is not a toothpaste or a mouthwash, but rather a chewable tablet designed to introduce billions of probiotic strains directly into the oral cavity. The creators operate on the premise that many common dental issues, such as cavities, gum disease, and bad breath, are not solely due to poor hygiene but also an imbalance in the oral microbiome, often exacerbated by modern diets and oral care products that strip away good bacteria.

ProDentim Ingredients and How It Works

Each chewable tablet delivers a blend of beneficial bacteria strains totaling 3.5 billion CFUs. The probiotic array includes Lactobacillus paracasei for gum support, Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04 for immune and respiratory support, and Lactobacillus reuteri, which is associated with reducing inflammation. These sit alongside a proprietary blend of plant-based ingredients and nutrients, including inulin, a prebiotic fiber that nourishes the beneficial bacteria; malic acid, found in strawberries and thought to help maintain tooth whiteness; tricalcium phosphate, a calcium source for tooth strength; and peppermint for refreshing, antimicrobial benefits. When chewed, the probiotics colonize the oral cavity, displacing harmful bacteria that contribute to tooth decay and gum inflammation.
