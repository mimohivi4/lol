How Does Joint Genesis Work?

Joint Genesis focuses on revitalizing the synovial fluid, often called "joint oil," which is crucial for smooth joint movement and shock absorption. As we age, the quality and quantity of this fluid diminish, leading to friction, wear, and discomfort. The supplement addresses this by replenishing synovial fluid, nourishing cartilage so it cushions between bones, reducing inflammation with anti-inflammatory ingredients, and protecting joint tissues from oxidative stress.
