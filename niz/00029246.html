What Is Joint Genesis?

Joint Genesis, developed by BioDynamix under Dr. Mark Weis, is a dietary supplement formulated to address age-related joint issues by replenishing hyaluronan, a key component of synovial fluid that cushions and lubricates joints. It combines natural, research-backed ingredients such as glucosamine, MSM, turmeric, collagen, Mobilee hyaluronan complex, and anti-inflammatory botanicals to support cartilage health, reduce friction, and promote flexibility.

Joint Genesis Ingredients

Joint Genesis prides itself on a blend of natural, safe ingredients, each scientifically selected for joint health. Key highlights include Mobilee, a patented extract rich in hyaluronic acid, collagen, and polysaccharides that supports joint lubrication; ginger root powder for anti-inflammatory support; Boswellia serrata, also known as Indian frankincense; pine bark extract for antioxidant protection; hyaluronic acid as a lubricant and shock absorber; and black pepper extract (BioPerine) to enhance absorption of the other ingredients.

Effectiveness

Users report improvements in joint comfort, mobility, and flexibility, with subtle benefits often noticed within 3-6 weeks and more significant results after 90-180 days of consistent use. Clinical studies on Mobilee suggest that 80mg/day can enhance joint function and comfort over time. The supplement is particularly recommended for individuals experiencing early to moderate age-related stiffness rather than severe arthritis.

Safety and Considerations

Joint Genesis is non-GMO, dairy-free, soy-free, and manufactured in FDA-approved, GMP-certified facilities. While generally safe, individuals taking blood thinners or anti-inflammatory medications should consult a physician due to mild blood-thinning effects from ingredients like ginger and boswellia. It is not recommended for children or pregnant women without medical advice.
