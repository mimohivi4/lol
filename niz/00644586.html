What Is ProDentim?

ProDentim is a dietary supplement specifically formulated to support the health of your teeth and gums by promoting a healthy balance of beneficial bacteria in the mouth. It is not a toothpaste or a mouthwash, but rather a chewable tablet designed to introduce billions of probiotic strains directly into the oral cavity. The premise is that many common dental issues, such as cavities, gum disease, and bad breath, are tied to an imbalance in the oral microbiome rather than poor hygiene alone.

Key Ingredients in the Formula

The core of the formula is 3.5 billion CFUs of probiotic strains, including Lactobacillus paracasei for gum health, Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04 for immune support, and Lactobacillus reuteri, associated with reduced inflammation. The probiotics are supported by inulin, a prebiotic fiber that feeds the beneficial bacteria; malic acid, believed to help maintain tooth whiteness; tricalcium phosphate for tooth strength; and peppermint for refreshing, antimicrobial qualities. This blend of natural ingredients is designed to outcompete harmful bacteria and support a healthier oral environment from within.

How ProDentim Works

When the tablet is chewed, the probiotics are released directly into the mouth, where they colonize the oral cavity. The beneficial bacteria work to displace harmful, decay-causing microbes, support a healthy inflammatory response in the gums, and restore the microbiome balance that discourages odor-causing compounds. A balanced oral environment also reduces acid production from harmful bacteria, which indirectly supports the integrity of tooth enamel.
