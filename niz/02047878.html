The mechanism behind The Genius Switch is brainwave entrainment. Two tones are played, one in each ear, and the brain notices the difference between them and naturally syncs to it. The reviewer compares it to physics rather than belief, and stresses that headphones are non-negotiable because of how the two tones must be delivered.
