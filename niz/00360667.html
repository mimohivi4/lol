What PermitPal Is

PermitPal is a digital permit guidance tool that helps homeowners, contractors, investors, and property managers check which permits a remodel may need. The process starts with your project: you enter your ZIP code, describe your remodel, and receive a report. The report may include a municipality-specific permit checklist, estimated permit fees, inspection guidance, material cost estimates, Home Depot and Lowe's links, inspector tips, and common mistake warnings.

Strengths of PermitPal

PermitPal's main strengths are its low one-time price, fast turnaround, and easy-to-follow reports. It helps organize permit guidance and cost details so homeowners can prepare before construction begins. Most homeowners use it because they want fewer surprises, better planning, and clearer costs, and the 30-day money-back guarantee makes it a low-risk option.

Complaints and Limitations

The most common limitations cited by users relate to the fact that PermitPal provides guidance and estimates rather than final approvals. Permit requirements vary by location, so a report for one ZIP code may not match another city's rules. Complaints usually center on homeowners who expect the report to be a complete, official answer, when it should instead be combined with confirmation from the local building department before work begins.

How to Use PermitPal Effectively

Start with the basics: know your project type, location, planned changes, and project size, and these details create a clearer permit check. Review the checklist items, which include remodel type, ZIP code, structural changes, electrical changes, plumbing changes, and local requirements. Then contact your local building office to confirm final requirements before spending money on materials or starting work, because skipping a rule does not remove it, and late fixes usually cost more.
