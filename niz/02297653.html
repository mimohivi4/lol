What Is PermitPal?

PermitPal is a digital guidance tool that helps homeowners find required permits before starting a remodel. Many remodels begin with a simple idea, such as a better kitchen, a new bathroom, or more living space, but every remodel changes your home. Some changes need permits, some need inspections, and some need approval before work begins. PermitPal helps organize permit guidance and cost details so homeowners can review their needs before construction starts.

Key Pros of PermitPal

PermitPal helps homeowners prepare before construction, which reduces surprises and improves budget planning. The process is simple: you enter your ZIP code, describe your remodel, and receive a report. The report may include a municipality-specific permit checklist, estimated permit fees, inspection guidance, material cost estimates, Home Depot and Lowe's links, inspector tips, and common mistake warnings. With a one-time report price of $9.99 and a 30-day money-back guarantee, it offers a low-cost way to check permit needs before starting work.

Key Cons and Limitations

PermitPal provides guidance and estimates rather than final approvals, so it does not replace your local building office. Permit requirements vary by location, and a report generated for one ZIP code or project type may not apply to another city or county. Homeowners who rely on a single general answer may miss local rules, application fees, or inspection steps, so the report should be used as a starting point alongside local building department confirmation.
