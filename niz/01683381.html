How It Works

Caffeine & CBD Synergy

Caffeine works by blocking adenosine receptors in the brain, preventing the onset of drowsiness and promoting alertness. However, this mechanism can also increase cortisol and adrenaline, leading to jitters and anxiety. CBD modulates the endocannabinoid system and interacts with serotonin receptors (5-HT1A) to counteract these overstimulation effects, creating a balanced energy experience.

Nano-Emulsification Technology

Traditional CBD oil doesn't mix well with water-based beverages like coffee. CBD Coffee uses nano-emulsified CBD, where CBD molecules are broken down to nanometer size and encapsulated in water-compatible carriers. This allows the CBD to dissolve completely and evenly in hot coffee, providing consistent dosing and rapid absorption.

Endocannabinoid System Activation

CBD interacts with CB1 and CB2 receptors throughout the body, helping regulate mood, stress response, and physical performance. When combined with caffeine's energy-promoting effects, the endocannabinoid system provides a stable foundation for sustained performance.
