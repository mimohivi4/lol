How It Works

Natural Testosterone Production Support

The formula provides nutritional cofactors and botanical compounds that support Leydig cell function in the testes -- the primary site of testosterone production. Ingredients like zinc, vitamin D3, and D-aspartic acid directly support the enzymatic pathways involved in T synthesis.

Aromatase Inhibition

The aromatase enzyme converts testosterone to estrogen. Certain compounds in the formula naturally inhibit this enzyme, helping maintain higher free testosterone levels without synthetic intervention.

CBD Cortisol Management

Cortisol and testosterone have an inverse relationship -- when cortisol is chronically elevated, testosterone production drops. CBD's powerful stress-modulating effects help normalize cortisol, removing a primary suppressor of testosterone.

Endocannabinoid-Hormonal Axis

The endocannabinoid system directly influences the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis that controls testosterone production. CBD optimizes this signaling pathway, supporting healthy hormone regulation at the source.
