What Is VisiFlora?

VisiFlora is a 22-ingredient daily capsule designed to support visual clarity, retinal protection and long-term eye comfort. What sets it apart from a standard lutein pill is its focus on the gut-eye axis: the emerging idea that lipopolysaccharide (LPS) toxins leaking from a compromised gut lining can enter the bloodstream and quietly inflame retinal tissue over time. The formula works across four blends - Vision Defense, the Gut-Eye Barrier, Vision Performance and Macular & Lens Protection - and is described as non-GMO, stimulant-free, 100% vegan and made in a USDA-certified organic facility.
